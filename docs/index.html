Keep your head in the fight

No stopping mid-pull to type. Say it in a breath and get back to it. Eight seconds of speech is ready in a third of a second.

Easier on your hands

If typing is painful, slow or tiring, talk instead. One button or one shortcut, and your words are ready to paste.

Controller or keyboard

Click the right stick on Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and more, or pick your own keyboard shortcut. Both are first-class.

Stays on your PC

Speech recognition runs on your own PC, and knows WoW's place names. Nothing you say is uploaded.

Say it, paste it, send it

With a controller
  1. LBRBDown

    Open chat

    As usual, or from the radial menu.

  2. RS

    Say it

    Click the right stick, wait for the beep, talk. Pause when you're done.

  3. CtrlV

    Paste it

    Ctrl+V into the chat box. Blizzard's rules mean the paste is yours. RS again cancels.

  4. A

    Send

    Check it, send it. B backs out.

Questions

What does it actually do in the game?

Nothing. No keys pressed, no addon, no game files touched. It listens for your button or shortcut, puts what you say on the clipboard, and you paste and send it. That's by design: Blizzard's rules don't allow software to send input to the game.

Who is it for?

Anyone who'd rather talk than type: players who don't want to stop mid-fight, controller players with no keyboard to hand, and anyone for whom typing is painful, slow or tiring.

Is it just for controllers?

No. Keyboard players get their own shortcut: press it, speak, then Enter, Ctrl+V, Enter. It works in any program, like Windows+H, but knows WoW's words.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

SmartScreen flags new apps, and the installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The code is open on GitHub, and the installer is built there.

Does my voice go anywhere?

No. Speech recognition (OpenAI's Whisper) runs on your PC. It only goes online to download a voice model and check for updates, which you can turn off.

Will it slow my game down?

Barely. The recommended model uses about 1 GB of memory and works a third of a second per message on a graphics card. No capable graphics card? A smaller model runs on the processor.

Can I paste with my controller?

Yes: map a spare button to Ctrl+V in your controller's software or Steam Input. Speak Forever won't press it for you, to stay within Blizzard's rules.

What do I need?

Windows 10 (2004+) or 11, 64-bit; WoW: Forever (beta works); a microphone, ideally a headset. No admin rights needed.
